I am a Research Scientist at the [A\*STAR Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/cfar), where I work in the Trustworthy AI team with Prof. Ivor Tsang.

My research studies how to make machine learning systems trustworthy when they are deployed in practice. Current directions include:

- **Fairness**: worst-case and Rawlsian fairness when demographic information is limited or unavailable.
- **Privacy**: privacy leakage in LLMs and vision-language models, differentially private data distillation, and data provenance.
- **Agentic AI safety**: risks of LLM agents, and auditing of agentic systems.

My work appears in NeurIPS, IJCAI, AAAI, TPAMI, JMLR, and TNNLS. I serve as a reviewer for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, TPAMI, and TNNLS.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Systems from the University of Technology Sydney in 2023, and my M.E. and B.E. in Computer Science from Northwestern Polytechnical University.
